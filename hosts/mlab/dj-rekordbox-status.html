How to make a stick
  1. Wait for Ready to copy.
  2. Format the stick as FAT32 once: sudo mkfs.vfat -F 32 -n FIESTA /dev/sdX
  3. Plug the stick into the laptop and click Replace with DJ library in the notification. It replaces /dj and /PIONEER on the stick and leaves everything else alone.
  4. Without the notification, run dj-stick sdb1 with the stick's partition.
